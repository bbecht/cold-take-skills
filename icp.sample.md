# Sample ICP

Replace every value below with your own. Keep the section headings. Archie reads them by name.

## Cutoff date

2026-01-01. Archie never evaluates an account created before this date. Set it to the day you switch Archie on.

## Qualifying condition

An account is In only if every clause is true:

- Established B2B company selling to other businesses
- Has a named owner or executive who makes the buying decision
- Has a problem your offer solves and no working solution in place
- Can fund the work

## Targeting filter

| | |
|---|---|
| Headcount | 25 to 150 |
| Revenue | $5M to $40M, where known |
| Buyer | VP or C-level in the function you serve |
| Geography | Your service region |

## Hard exclusions

Out if any is true:

- Outside your service region
- Already has an in-house team doing the work you sell
- No identifiable or reachable decision maker
- Direct competitor

## Green flags

Raise confidence. Never override an exclusion.

- Hired a new leader in your function in the last 12 months
- Raised funding or announced expansion in the last 12 months
- Open job postings for roles your offer replaces

## Superseded, do not reintroduce

List rules you have retired so the agent never brings them back.

- (example) Industry-only gate, dropped in favor of the qualifying condition
